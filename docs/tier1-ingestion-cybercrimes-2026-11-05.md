The two laws are separate instruments, as with Minimum Wage. The booklet also holds the 2024 Act, but one source cannot belong to two instruments, so the 2024 text comes from the PLAC copy. The two 2024 copies were compared word by word and agree, apart from OCR noise.

## Corrections, 2015 Act

- **Fine amounts:** all 2015 fine amounts were checked by eye against page crops. One was a serious misread: s.33(16) is ₦1,000,000, not ₦11,000,000. The struck ₦ was also restored throughout.
- **First Schedule:** retyped from the page. The crop had clipped line ends, for example "Traffic in Persons", and misread letters.
- **Page 31 (ss.45-48):** this page is badly printed. Corrected: `Lat` to Law, `release` to relevant, `wader` to under, `sho` to who, `las` to has, `(zl)` to (d) in s.45(3), and `(m)` to (a) in s.48(1). s.46(a) keeps the print's "willfully".
- **Punctuation:** a stop before a lower-case word was restored to a comma. A colon closing a list item or definition was restored to a semicolon, because the semicolon tail is faint in the scan.
- **Definitions:** opening quotation marks restored in ss.26 and 58, and list letters corrected in "Access Device".
- **Running headers:** removed from section text.
- **Rejected items:** the 6 rejections are sub-lists inside s.58 definitions. The section text keeps them.

## Kept as printed and flagged, 2015 Act

- s.3: the second subsection has no printed "(2)".
- s.6(3) "or both such"; s.15(2) "A person steals"; s.15(3) "not more ₦1,000,000.00"; s.22(3) "not more 5 years"; s.23(1) "Commit offence".
- s.17(2) "genuiness". The 2024 Act tries to correct it but quotes it as "geniuses".
- s.24(5) "discharged a further order".
- s.26(1)(b)(i) "national of ethnic origin"; s.26(2) "imprisonment torture".
- s.33(10) ₦500,000 is printed without kobo, unlike the other amounts.
- s.40(4) refers to "section 20", which looks wrong for the subject.
- s.41(3) "training programmers".
- s.58 "shown of it" in "Expired Card".
- ss.8 and 16(3) are near duplicates, as are ss.14(7) and 27(2). Both pairs are as printed.

## Corrections, 2024 Amendment

- s.1: closing bracket added after "the Principal Act". The print never closes it, and the blocking punctuation issue was cleared on the merits. This is the one printed misprint corrected.
- The other eight revisions restore commas, dashes, the letter (j) and a speck that OCR had misread.

## Kept as printed and flagged, 2024 Amendment

- s.2(a) targets the word "geniuses", but the Principal Act prints "genuiness".
- s.3 opens with "Section 21 (1)" but also amends subsection (3).
- s.11 opens "Section of 44".
- s.11(a) replaces paragraph (a) "in subsection (1)", but the levy is at s.44(2)(a). s.11(b)'s new subsection (8) correctly cites s.44(2)(a).
- s.12 and s.13 have no closing stop.

## Currency

The 2015 text is as enacted, not current. The 2024 Act changes ss.17, 21, 22, 24, 27(2), 30, 37(1)(a), 38, 41(1), 44 and 48. Nigeria Tax Act s.196(14) also deletes s.44(2)(a), s.44(4) and the Second Schedule, so the levy text is doubly affected.

Reader notices were added on ss.17, 21, 22, 24, 27, 30, 37, 38, 41, 44 and 48 and on the Second Schedule, as on Minimum Wage s.3. The Pidgin, Yoruba, Hausa and Igbo strings are unreviewed drafts.

## Limits of the check

Every 2015 page had three Vision passes, and the full text was read through by hand. No independent copy of the 2015 wording exists: the asianlaws text is a differently worded bill. Agreement between passes is therefore the only cross-check for words, and punctuation rests on context as well as the image.

The OCR-pass comparison that exposes the ₦11,000,000 error was also run on VAPP and FCCPA. Neither showed a disputed amount in the body of the law.
