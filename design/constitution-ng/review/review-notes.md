## Assessment

**A — recommended.** The most useful combination of a memorable initial, open form and flag reference. The split remains visible at 16 px, although a dedicated pixel-tuned favicon should be refined during the kit stage. Its flag connection is suggestive rather than a literal rectangle.

**B.** Strongest direct three-band structure and simplest silhouette. Its meaning as source quotation suits the evidence-focused product. The risk is familiarity: quotation punctuation is widely used, so it offers less ownership than A.

**C.** Readable compact ng construction and a more literary wordmark. The Nigerian connection comes mainly through ng and colour. At 16 px its shared stem is denser than A/B; 24 px and above is preferable for unambiguous letter reading.

All full-name lockups are wide because of the long domain name. Use symbol-only at favicon sizes. The approved kit should include a stacked lockup, a dedicated small drawing and reversed optical corrections.
