# constitution.ng — logo exploration

Date: 12 October 2026. Direction A, **Open Clause**, was selected by Amos. Its refined artwork and deliverables are in `logo-kit/`; the exploration below is retained as the decision record.

## Confirmed brief

- Exact brand name: **constitution.ng**, lowercase, including the domain suffix.
- Personality: calm, credible and approachable.
- Nigerian identity: clear green and white.
- Product: a conversational guide to the Nigerian Constitution, with plain-language explanations and traceable source provisions.
- Primary audience assumption: everyday Nigerians; secondary audiences include students and legal researchers.
- First applications: website navigation, browser tab, social avatar and source-sharing materials.
- Decision-maker: Amos.

The promise is **constitutional knowledge within reach**. The identity should feel welcoming while treating source text seriously. The first round compares three different structures in black and white, with a small green-and-white study after selection of a recommendation. No tagline is needed in the logo.

## Strategy and research

Avoid generic gavels, scales, shields, official seals, literal country outlines, AI sparkles and a stock chat bubble. Green-white-green is the national cue; the name and a distinctive silhouette provide recognition.

Adjacent products reviewed: [LawPavilion/Primsol](https://primsol.lawpavilion.com/features), [LawPàdí](https://lawpadi.com/), [EiE Nigeria](https://eie.ng/), and [BudgIT](https://budgit.org/our_programs/civichive/). These are category references, not sources of artwork. The project itself already uses emerald, Public Sans and Source Serif 4.

The bundled logo library's education category contains 23 marks; blue and geometric sans typography are frequent. Construction studies: Pagekit (compact negative space), Framer (few primitives), Ello (large counter), Khan Academy (friendly silhouette), and Flarum/Font Awesome (flag resemblance checks). None was traced or reused.

Word map:

- Constitution → clause, source, quotation, shared agreement, enduring text.
- Access → opening, visible passage, open counter, invitation.
- Nigeria → green-white-green, .ng, shared civic ground.
- Credible → proportion, stable baseline, deliberate space, quiet typography.
- Approachable → lowercase, open counters, gentle curves.

## Construction intentions

A: a broad round C on a 256-unit grid, with a real vertical opening through its body; widen the opening if it closes at 16 px. Preserve an unmistakable C silhouette and calm curved terminals.

B: two matching filled quote/page shapes separated by an equal central band. Each has one rounded shoulder and one 45-degree cut. This ties the Nigerian flag to exact quotation rather than courtroom imagery.

C: hand-drawn lowercase n and g share a central stem. The open n counter and enclosed g counter contrast while the descending g gives the compact mark a distinctive footprint. Pair with quieter editorial lettering.

## Boundaries

Only design files are part of this exploration. Existing application files and unrelated working changes are outside this task. The chosen identity needs a professional trademark/resemblance search before launch; these studies are not legal clearance or confirmation of domain availability.
