What we heard

The brief

A free, plain-language guide to the Constitution of the Federal Republic of Nigeria. Ask a question in your own words and get the exact section, explained the way a knowledgeable friend would. It must feel friendly, proudly Nigerian and precise, and never like an official government seal.

friendlyplain-spokenproudly Nigerianmodernprecise

Success: reads at 16 px in a browser tab, works in one colour, avoids the category's scales, gavels, pillars and coat-of-arms imagery, and stays clearly independent of government branding.

Final design

Open Book

The Constitution as an open book that talks back: its green pages and white gutter echo the flag, and the gap above the gutter forms the Niger–Benue Y.

  • Says what the product does in one glance: read the law, and ask it questions
  • Green–white–green without drawing a flag; the confluence Y rewards a second look
  • Custom lowercase wordmark: round, open letters with hooked t's, so it reads warm rather than official
  • A dedicated small-size cut keeps the gutter and tail visible at 16 px
